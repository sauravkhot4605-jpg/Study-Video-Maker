---
name: Teaching depth
description: What deeper knowledge means for Studyloom lessons and what quality checks cannot guarantee.
---

Improve depth in the spoken lesson itself, not only in a separate study guide. Explain prerequisites, why a method or claim works, justified intermediate steps, limitations, misconceptions and transfer to a new example.

**Why:** The user asked for deeper knowledge and better explanations after the animation improvements. More motion or a longer transcript alone does not address that need.

**How to apply:** Keep the core worked example and reasoning consistent across narration, visuals and the learning guide. Longer lessons should add substantive applications and comparisons rather than repetition.

Treat schema/coverage/word-count checks as structural safeguards, not factual verification.

**Why:** Fluent, long AI output can still contain incorrect facts or reasoning. A complete guide is evidence of coverage, not proof of accuracy.

**How to apply:** Preserve source fidelity, distinguish illustrative examples from source claims, never fabricate citations or claim fact-checking, and keep any automatic content-depth revision bounded to avoid uncontrolled cost.
