# Studyloom — Make it make sense

## Director's treatment
- **Promise:** A tangled study topic can become a clear, memorable lesson, one small idea at a time.
- **Identity:** Studyloom's warm-paper / deep-green / marigold / coral language; Manrope-like bold display paired with DM Sans utility typography.
- **Hero:** A single thread of coral ink that travels through the film, becoming a question mark, a lesson path, a diagram, and finally a woven Studyloom mark.
- **Motion:** Thread-led morph cuts; punchy masked typography; tactile paper-card choreography with gentle depth.
- **Arc:** Confusion arrives as a dense knot, gets pulled into a simple sequence, becomes an animated lesson, then resolves into knowledge the viewer can keep.
- **Payoff:** The ending does not repeat the interface reveal: every piece of the lesson folds into a quiet, singular thread-mark and the promise.
- **Format/runtime:** 16:9 landscape, 30 seconds.

## Full shot script

### Shot 01 / 0.00–4.80s / 4.80s — “TOO MUCH?”
**Purpose:** Recognize the familiar overwhelm of a dense topic, then reveal that a clear beginning is possible.

**Exact content and holds:** “A whole chapter?” appears at 0.25s and holds to 1.05s; “Three pages of notes?” at 1.05s to 1.90s; “TOO MUCH?” takes over at 2.00s and holds until 4.20s. Small wordmark “studyloom” appears at 3.20s and stays through 4.80s. No voiceover.

**Composition/artistic idea:** Tight, almost overhead crop on a deep pine-green field. Uneven ivory note fragments with tiny legible fragments (“photosynthesis”, “cellular energy”, “chlorophyll”) pile at frame edges; a large 22vmin “TOO MUCH?” owns the center-left. A coral thread visibly tangles around the question mark, then finds a clean exit. Cream light from top-left; no generic texture overlay.

**Timed choreography:** 0.00–0.25s: a paper edge and coral thread already visible, no empty incoming frame. 0.25s: first note punches in from depth; 0.70s: second note collides and pivots. 1.05s: first phrase flips to second as scraps drift outward. 1.90–2.15s: “TOO MUCH?” snaps in on a stiff spring; note fragments recoil. 2.60s: the question mark's coral thread tightens once. 3.20s: small Studyloom name stamps in. 3.90–4.80s: thread pulls toward frame center and scales up into the next shot; text shears away with it.

**Transition contract:** The outgoing thread ends at 52%/53% frame position; shot 02 begins with that same coral line at the same point. Combine thread scale-through with a cream paper panel unfolding from the center.

**Assets/technique:** CSS paper slips and custom SVG thread/question mark, masked title, motion depth. Existing app brand references inform palette; no downloaded logo or external assets.

**Sound:** The 100 BPM instrumental pulse continues; the 4.80s cut lands on beat 9 by construction (per-beat drum accent; not RMS-analyzed).

### Shot 02 / 4.80–10.20s / 5.40s — “START WITH ONE THING.”

The user input is rendered as a single oversized ruled line, not a full UI recreation. Coral thread behaves like a pencil underline; handwritten-like strokes are geometric paths, not script fonts.

**Timed choreography:** 0.00–0.35s: center thread scale-through reveals a paper plane and ink settles. 0.35–1.20s: letters of “How do volcanoes form?” type into the ruled strip, 70ms each. 1.40s: the line curves into a prompt-card corner; “A topic.” appears. 1.80–2.60s: card folds in half and unfolds with “Your own notes.” on the reverse. 3.00s: three miniature note strokes pour from its edge. 3.40s: “Start with one thing.” masks on, one word per 150ms. 4.25–5.40s: underline races toward the right edge, pulls all paper into a rotating page turn.

**Transition contract:** The curled page corner becomes the first of three lesson cards in shot 03. The transition combines a page-turn perspective and a coral thread draw that bridges the cut.

**Assets/technique:** CSS paper planes, staggered text, SVG stroke underline, perspective transform and clipped page fold. All copy stays inside 5% safe inset.

**Sound:** Instrumental continues; the 10.20s cut lands on beat 18 by construction (per-beat drum accent; not RMS-analyzed).

## Variety and pacing review
The film moves from cropped typographic disorder to a single ruled input, an overhead three-card choreography, a centered lesson diagram, a diagonal takeaway stack, and a spacious centered brand lockup. Each adjacent transition changes at least two of framing, field, hero scale, and mechanic, while the coral thread carries a physical visual cue throughout. Every shot contains post-reveal events and an outgoing motion in its final 15–20%; opener events change at sub-second intervals. Claims are limited to project facts: topic/text input, animated lesson, AI narration, key terms, and takeaways.
